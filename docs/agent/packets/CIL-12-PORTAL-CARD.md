# CIL-12 — Portal verdict card

## Why

A verdict nobody can see will not change a showing or an offer.

## Units

- **A** — Small card on deal, person-property, and property cockpit:
  pick, dissent, kill, next, evidence kinds, outcome.
- **B** — Brand navy/gold, editorial, no persona names, no scorecard.
- **C** — Read through `council.getBySubject`. No client SQL.
- **D** — iPad touch targets ~48px. Do not nest inside a markdown table.

## Acceptance

- Card renders from a fixture verdict without calling a model.
- Empty state is "no decision" not an error.
- Public `/buyers` does not show the card.

## Not this story

- No new visual language.
